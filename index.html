Dunedin Paving – Pool Deck Paver Installation

When it comes to choosing a pool decking material, paving stones are the clear winner. Not only do they offer incredible slip-resistance and mimic the look of natural stone but also come in an abundance of colors and designs! Through carefully mixing and matching different types of pavers, you can craft personalised designs that perfectly match your desired aesthetic – whether contemporary or rustic. With so many options available, there really is something for everyone!

The groundwork for a successful paver installation is the preparation of the base. To ensure that pavers adhere to their sub-base properly, make sure it’s level and solid. If necessary, coping stones should be installed with a 1-inch overhang and set in place using mortar – if possible, use complementary mortar joints. For auto covers with tracks however, construction adhesive should replace mortar application on the track portion before installing your pavers.

Before you start the process of designing a pool deck, consider the full patio design and planter beds as well. Mark out boundaries with string line, chalk, or lime to form an accurate representation of your measurements before buying pavers – if in doubt about exact requirements ask a paver dealer for advice on style, pattern and amount required.
